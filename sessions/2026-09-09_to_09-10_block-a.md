## Decisions Aditya made

- **Blocks supersede.** Each zip replaces the last for new claims. The earlier
  export was deleted on 09-10 after it turned out to be a *different campaign*
  — 28 of 48 receptors shared, no shared prediction paths, different arms.

  during housekeeping, not by the brief.
- **No lettered panels in the graphical abstract.** Rebuilt as one composition,
  then four alternative styles commissioned in parallel.

## What the verification found

Four were named in the brief; **eighteen were found here**. Five would have put
something false or unsupported in the paper:

- `8FZQ.cif` is CFTR, not an opioid complex — the most on-thesis render in the
  drop cannot be built (D18)
- the "confidently wrong" structure is confidently *right* — an apo prediction
  0.95 Å from the inactive reference (D12)
- SC-1's "cluster bootstrap" intervals are the receptor column, understating the
  width (D5)
- four `ALIGNMENT.md` files name anchors that do not reproduce (D13, D20)
- `rmsd_to_active_ref` does not reproduce from coordinates and the scorer's atom
  set is documented nowhere (D22)

## What I got wrong and corrected

- Asserted in Methods that **MSAs were pinned**. No template or MSA column
  exists in the 55-column table; nothing claims it. Removed.
- Published that **chiesa2025templatebias has no apo arm**. It does — the
  AFM-receptor protocol — and both its arms are ligand-free because AF2 cannot
  take small molecules. Withdrawn.
- Left Block A's prose as markdown outside the manuscript, so the paper had no
  Block A section at all until it was `.tex`.
- Our own `camera.py` bent the membrane normal by **35.5°** on every DRD2 render
  shipped before 09-10, by fitting the axis through a 147-residue pLDDT-38 ICL3.
