## Resolved before the session ended

`paper-6f` verified and fixed four of the findings, withdrew one as its own error, and held four for
Aditya. The decomposition is the important one: **the honest analysis is two terms, not three, and
they telescope exactly** — 0.400 + 0.333 = 0.7332 = apo→cognate. The clean α5-CT contrast
(`decoy → cognate`, +0.333 [0.242, 0.432] over 24 clusters, independently bootstrapped by both
sessions) is **larger** than the confounded term it replaces. Removing the confound did not cost the
effect; it increased it.

`intro.tex` was restated on that basis: the decomposition is now described in two steps, and the
family question is stated as "separate and weaker" rather than as a third component, which matches
SC-B-2's CI crossing zero on 3 of 4 backbones.

### The BC-1 scope question, closed

It went through three states in an hour and the middle one was wrong in both directions.

1. My SI caption said the "apo arm alone" scope was **false**, from the shipped `n_rows` of 2,800
   against an apo-only expectation of 1,400.
2. `paper-6f` could not find `n_rows` in that JSON and edited the panel to say the scope was
   **unconfirmable** — the artefact carries no row counts and the census is a superset.
3. Checked both claims. **`n_rows` is in the file**, four times, one per backbone, exactly the
   values cited. And `paper-6f` was right that I had missed a column: the census role field is
   `role`, not `ligand_role`, which is what I had tested for.

That column closed it. The design grid is **exactly 50 rows per receptor × backbone × role × arm** —
unique size 50 across all 40,000 census rows, no cell of any other size. So apo-only would be
28 × 50 = 1,400 and 23 × 50 = 1,150 per backbone; the shipped counts are 2,800 and 2,300 on every
backbone. Exactly twice. Both arms pooled.

The superset objection does not apply: the census is not being asked which receptors the 2×2 used,
only what the grid constant is, and that constant holds for any subset drawn from it.

**Both of us were wrong about one checkable thing, and both were checkable in about a minute** —
a traversal that stopped early, and a column name tested in the wrong spelling. The caption now
says *contradicted*, with the arithmetic in it.

This also demotes `rows.tier3.v2.csv` for this question: it is no longer needed to know the scope
statement is wrong, only to know what the correct apo-only result would be. It remains ask 1 for
the G4 gate.
