\caption{\textbf{The partner acts as a switch, not a dial.}
\textbf{a}, the fraction of seeds the predicate calls active, per receptor
$\times$ backbone cell. The distribution is bimodal at both ends:
\textbf{111 of 160 apo cells never fire on any of 25 seeds, and 108 of 159
cognate cells fire on all 25.} \textbf{b}, the same cells paired, apo against
cognate, with the identity line labelled; 120 of 159 move up, 37 are unchanged
and 2 move down, both named. \textbf{c}, the full receptor $\times$ backbone
grid for each arm on a shared colour scale, receptors ordered by mean change.

\textit{Filter:} E1+E2, then Class~A only --- 7{,}966 of 9{,}490 rows, forming
319 cells and 159 receptor $\times$ backbone pairs with both arms.}

\begin{figure}[htbp]\centering
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{ba4_amplitude.pdf}
\caption{\textbf{Receptor-specific activation amplitude is not reproduced.}
\textbf{a}, predicted shift against reference separation on the NPxxY axis,
with the fitted line, its cluster-bootstrap band, and \textbf{the unity line ---
the slope a model reproducing amplitude would show --- drawn on every panel}.
\textbf{b}, slopes on both axes under three inclusion sets, with zero and unity
both marked; filled circles exclude zero, open circles include it. Protenix2 on
NPxxY excludes zero at a slope of 0.26, roughly a quarter of unity.
\textbf{c}, restriction of range: the tilt predictor's SD of 1.17~\AA{} beside
NPxxY's 5.22~\AA. \textbf{d}, attenuation sensitivity with unstable regions
shaded --- 44 of 84 swept points are unstable on tilt against 0 of 84 on NPxxY,
which is why no corrected tilt slope is reported.
\textit{Filter:} \texttt{class\_a\_only} with per-axis E3; $n = 28$ receptors
(NPxxY) and 32 (tilt).}
\end{figure}
