\begin{figure}[htbp]\centering
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{bb12_ladder_decomposition.pdf}
\caption{\textbf{The four-arm ladder, and the two contrasts it decomposes into.}
\textbf{a}, two-instrument active-call fraction across the four input arms, on
each backbone and pooled over the panel with cluster-bootstrap intervals, with
the two clean steps bracketed beneath. The decoy arm carries the correct
G$\alpha$ subunit with its eleven C-terminal residues permuted; the shuffled arm
carries an intact subunit from a different G$\alpha$ family; the cognate arm
carries the correct subunit. \textbf{Every partner arm supplies a complete
G$\alpha$ subunit.} Supplying any partner at all, even one whose $\alpha$5 tail
is permuted, accounts for $+0.400$ [0.334, 0.464]; restoring the correct
C-terminal sequence accounts for the remaining $+0.333$ [0.242, 0.432], and the
two sum to the apo-to-cognate total exactly. \textbf{b}, the same fractions on
the logit scale, because the probability scale saturates at cognate. \textbf{c},
what each contrast actually varies, reported as an effect estimate with its
interval. \textbf{No panel in the 81-paper survey behind this work reports a
co-folding contrast that way}; the nearest analogues state a difference in the
text and draw no test statistic, effect size or interval for it, and that is the
gap this panel is meant to close rather than a convention borrowed from another
field. Grey marks what is not the subject of a contrast, in \textbf{a} and
\textbf{c} alike.
\textbf{The decoy-to-cognate step is the only contrast in this work that
isolates the $\alpha$5 C-terminus}: the two arms carry the same subunit, of the
same length, from the same family, differing only in eleven C-terminal residues
and differing there by a permutation, so composition is held constant. The
campaign's shipped middle term, decoy-to-shuffled, is named
\texttt{delta\_a5ct\_sequence\_decoy\_to\_shuffled} and is \emph{not} an
$\alpha$5-CT contrast --- the shuffled arm is a different G$\alpha$ family, so
that step changes the tail, the scaffold, the length and the family at once. It
is drawn in grey in \textbf{c} and named confounded. The family term
($+0.082$) sits \emph{inside} the $\alpha$5-CT step, not beside it.
\textit{Filter:} Class~A, frame\_36 --- 36 receptors, \textbf{24} paralog
clusters, not the 26 that every shipped frame\_36 interval is labelled with;
EDNRA, EDNRB, GRPR and HRH3 are excluded because position 7.53 is Leu, not Tyr,
so the NPxxY axis is undefined. The predicate is recomputed from every row
rather than read from a shipped call column, and the per-backbone rates and the
decomposition terms are computed by two independent routes; the panel refuses to
draw unless they agree on all four rungs.}
\label{fig:bb12}
\end{figure}
