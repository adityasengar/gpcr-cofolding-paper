\begin{figure}[htbp]\centering
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{ba1_reference_landscape.pdf}
\caption{\textbf{The instrument and its calibration.}
Both predicates scored on the deposited reference structures, where the answer is
already known. \textbf{a}, NPxxY hydroxyl distance against TM6 tilt, coloured by
annotated state, with both thresholds drawn. \textbf{b}, the same faceted by
receptor class: Class~A separates, Class~B and~F do not, which is the
justification for restricting quantitative claims to Class~A. \textbf{c},
per-receptor reference separation on both axes, sorted --- this pre-loads the
restriction-of-range argument that pays off in Fig.~4. Deviating entries are
labelled with their receptor and marked by deviation class; five of the nine
deviations are unclassified as to cause and carry a neutral marker rather than
being folded into a named class.
\textit{Filter:} references as shipped; $n = 167$ unique entries (98 on the
48-receptor panel).}
\end{figure}

\begin{figure}[htbp]\centering
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{ba4_amplitude.pdf}
\caption{\textbf{Receptor-specific activation amplitude is not reproduced.}
\textbf{a}, predicted shift against reference separation on the NPxxY axis, four
small multiples, fitted line with interval and \textbf{the unity line drawn on
every panel}. \textbf{b}, slopes on both axes with zero and unity both marked;
filled circles exclude zero, open circles include it. Protenix2 on NPxxY excludes
zero and is drawn as such. \textbf{c}, restriction of range: the tilt predictor's
SD of 1.17~\AA{} beside NPxxY's 5.22~\AA. \textbf{d}, attenuation sensitivity,
with unstable regions shaded --- 44 of 84 swept points are unstable on tilt
against 0 of 84 on NPxxY, which is why no corrected tilt slope is reported.
\textit{Filter:} \texttt{class\_a\_only}; per-axis E3; $n = 28$ receptors
(NPxxY), 32 (tilt).}
\end{figure}
