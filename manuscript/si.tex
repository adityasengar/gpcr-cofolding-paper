\section{Scope of this document}

Every panel and table here states the exact row filter applied and the $n$ after
filtering. The exclusion flags in the shipped data are five independent sets
with different causes and different scopes; they are never applied as a union.
Applying the union would remove 5{,}093 of 9{,}490 rows (54\%) and would change
every distribution shown in the main text. The default filter throughout is
E1+E2, which removes 29 rows (0.31\%).

Discrepancies between the internal claim sheet and the shipped data are recorded
in full in \texttt{analysis/block\_a/DISCREPANCY\_REPORT.md}; the ones that
change a reported number are noted in the relevant caption below.

\paragraph{What was supplied, and what was not.}
\textbf{Every co-input arm reported in this work supplies a complete G$\alpha$
subunit of 350--394 residues, or a nanobody. No arm supplies a peptide of any
length.} The quantity actually varied between the decoy and cognate arms is the
\textbf{eleven} C-terminal residues of the $\alpha$5 helix, permuted rather than
replaced, so composition is held constant. Readers should hold that against the
title, which names a 21-residue peptide: the reduction to an isolated peptide is
motivated by this work and is not tested by it. This is flagged in the main text
as an open decision \textbf{[PI]} and is not resolved here.

\section{The co-input ladder: apo, decoy, shuffled, cognate}

Every arm in this block supplies a \textbf{complete G$\alpha$ subunit of 350--394
residues}. No arm supplies a peptide. The decoy arm supplies the \emph{correct}
subunit whose eleven C-terminal residues have been permuted --- composition held
constant, only the tail moved; the shuffled arm supplies a \emph{different}
G$\alpha$ class carrying its own native $\alpha$5 C-terminus.

\section{What the prior literature does, and does not, do}

These five panels summarise the 81-paper corpus assembled for this work. They

corpus, which is why it is calibrated against structures of known state in the main text
rather than asserted here.
\textit{Source:} corpus notes; every assignment carries the note text it was read

printed rather than one}: the choice between row, receptor and cluster bootstraps
has been mis-stated more than once during this work, and a table showing a single
