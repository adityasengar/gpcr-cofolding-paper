\section{Scope of this document}

Every panel and table here states the exact row filter applied and the $n$ after
filtering. The exclusion flags in the shipped data are five independent sets
with different causes and different scopes; they are never applied as a union.
Applying the union would remove 5{,}093 of 9{,}490 rows (54\%) and would change
every distribution shown in the main text. The default filter throughout is
E1+E2, which removes 29 rows (0.31\%).

Discrepancies between the internal claim sheet and the shipped data are recorded
in full in \texttt{analysis/block\_a/DISCREPANCY\_REPORT.md}; the ones that
change a reported number are noted in the relevant caption below.

\caption{\textbf{The exclusion sets, and why they are not one filter.}
Coverage of each flag, and every headline statistic under every exclusion
combination as a percentage shift from baseline. No sign flip occurs anywhere and
no statistic moves by more than 0.5\%. This panel is about the filters, not about
the values: what is plotted is stability, not magnitude.
\textit{Filter:} the sweep is the filter; each cell states its own set.}

\begin{figure}[htbp]\centering

\caption{\textbf{Reference-set audit.} Method, resolution, deposition date and
construct engineering across the reference entries, with entries whose

(\texttt{engineered\_mutation\_count}, \texttt{engineered\_mutation\_positions},

\textit{Filter:} references as shipped; $n = 167$ entries.}

\begin{figure}[htbp]\centering
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{s3_bootstrap_comparison.pdf}
\caption{\textbf{Cluster versus receptor bootstrap.} The cluster bootstrap over
26 paralog clusters is authoritative; the receptor bootstrap is shown here only.
The cluster interval is equal to or wider on every headline statistic, by a
factor of up to 2.2 --- not the $\sim$1.1$\times$ previously assumed. Eleven of
the 26 clusters are singletons, so the paralogy correction acts on 15
multi-member clusters.
\textit{Filter:} E1+E2; 1{,}000 resamples, seed 20260909.}
\end{figure}

\begin{figure}[htbp]\centering
