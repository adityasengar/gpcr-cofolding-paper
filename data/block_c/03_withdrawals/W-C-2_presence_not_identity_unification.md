# W-C-2 — "Presence not identity" unification of Block B + Block C

**Retracted**: unification claim from earlier drafts —
"these models read presence, not identity, at both receptor interfaces."

**Reason**: Block C alone tests identity (Tier 3 dropped the `none` arm
that would have tested presence). The cross-block correlation on
receptor-level contributions is Spearman ρ = 0.129 with 95 % CI
[−0.213, +0.443] at n = 35 — straddles zero.

**Correction**: the two interfaces solve different subproblems (per
`docs/BLOCK_C_PAPER_DRAFT_v1.md §Discussion`):
- Intracellular side (Block B): shape-complementarity of a helical
  anchor into a preformed cavity, near-native partner mass sufficient
  regardless of anchor sequence beyond ~11 residues.
- Orthosteric pocket (Block C): ligand-shape geometry against a
  chemistry-sensitive pocket. Identity DOES matter at Cα resolution
  (SC-C-1), though NOT at the binary predicate (SC-C-6, C-C-1).
