# W-8 — "14.5% panel mean as a cross-backbone signal"

## Claim as previously stated

The "14.5% apo-active panel mean" phrasing has appeared multiple times
in the campaign narrative as if it were a shared cross-backbone quantity.

## Retraction

**Withdrawn.** Same class of finding as W-1. 14.5% is the panel-mean
apo-active rate for **Protenix alone** on Block A. Cross-backbone
averaging over the four gave the appearance of a shared quantity that
in fact lives on one backbone. See W-1 for the per-backbone breakdown
and the honest sentence.

**Do not cross-backbone-average apo-active rates.** Report per-backbone
distributions. If a single number is needed, quote the median across
backbones and label it as such, not as a pooled rate.
