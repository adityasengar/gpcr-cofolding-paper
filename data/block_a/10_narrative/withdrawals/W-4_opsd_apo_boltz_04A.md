# W-4 — "OPSD/apo/Boltz reaches 0.40 Å from active crystal" as a cell-level median

## Claim as previously stated

Prior campaign framing quoted OPSD/apo/Boltz landing at 0.40 Å from the
active crystal reference — a striking value.

## Retraction

**Withdrawn as a cell-median claim; retained as a seed-min claim.** The
0.40 Å figure is the *seed-min* — the closest of the 25 samples in that
cell. The cell-median sample lands at 1.07 Å from active.

Both numbers are true; only one is the cell-level statistic.

## Evidence

- `dossiers/BLOCK_A/EXPERIMENT_DOSSIER_BLOCK_A.md` §Phase 5b (structural inspection of the median-representative CIF)
- CIF at `structures/block_a/curated/cifs/OPSD__apo__boltz__OPSD_apo_boltz__seed<N>.cif`

## What the manuscript should say instead

> OPSD/apo/Boltz produced a sample landing 0.40 Å from the active
> reference (seed-min across 25 samples). The cell-median sample lands
> at 1.07 Å. Both numbers are stable across seeds within the seed's
> five-sample cluster.
