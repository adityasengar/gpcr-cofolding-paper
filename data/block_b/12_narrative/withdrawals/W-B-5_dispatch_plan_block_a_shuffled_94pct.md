## Claim as previously stated

`docs/BLOCK_B_DISPATCH_PLAN_2026_09_02.md` line 105 asserts "Shuffled at
94% active in Block A" and threads that figure into subsequent Block B
framing.

## Retraction

**Withdrawn.** Block A had **no shuffled arm**. Per PREREG §12, Block A
ran the apo + cognate arms only; shuffled and decoy arms were reserved
for Block B. There is no Block A shuffled active fraction to cite.

The 94% referent that Phase 3 §3g and Phase 6 §94%-reconciliation surface
is **Block A protenix cognate active-call fraction = 94.4%** on Class A
n=36 two-instrument (`campaign_completion_report.md` line 240) — not
shuffled. Additionally, `campaign_completion_report.md` §1 records
Block A cognate normalised-position at ~94% (position on a continuous
axis, not activation fraction).

The three surfaced "94%" referents on repo-wide grep map to:

## What supersedes it

If the manuscript cites 94% for Block A, it must attribute the figure
to protenix cognate active-call on Class A n=36 two-instrument. Do NOT
attribute a 94% figure to Block A shuffled.
